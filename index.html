How to find a good software developer in Kenya

By Jeff Mutethia, 17 November 2026

Hiring a developer is one of the most important decisions for a new business website or app. A good one saves you money and brings in customers. A bad one leaves you with a slow site you don’t control. Here is how to choose well.

1. Know what you need first

Write down what the site must do: show your services, take bookings, sell products, accept M-Pesa. A clear list lets you compare quotes fairly and stops the project growing without agreement.

2. Look at live work, not just screenshots

Ask for links to real websites. Open them on your phone. Check that they load quickly, look right on a small screen and work properly. Most of your customers will visit on mobile.

3. Check experience in your industry

A developer who has built booking sites for tour operators will understand your needs faster than someone who has only built blogs. Ask what they have built that is close to your business.

4. Ask the right questions

  • Who owns the code, the domain and the hosting account?
  • Can you integrate M-Pesa or other payment gateways?
  • How will the site be set up to appear in Google search?
  • What happens after launch if something breaks?
  • How long will it take, and how often will I see progress?

5. Agree scope, price and timeline in writing

A short written agreement should list the pages and features, the total cost, the payment schedule and the delivery date. Pay in stages tied to progress, not everything upfront.

6. Watch for red flags

  • No live examples, or examples they can’t explain.
  • A price far below everyone else’s.
  • Pressure to pay in full before any work is shown.
  • No clear answer on who owns the website.
  • Slow or vague replies before you’ve even hired them.

7. Choose someone you can talk to

Your website will need updates over time. Pick a developer who explains things simply, answers quickly and is still reachable after the launch.
